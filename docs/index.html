From October 24th to November 8th 2025, CJLO 1690AM is once again hosting our annual Funding Drive! We have big plans for this year’s drive which includes special events hosted around the city and a goal of $15,000!

By hosting a Funding Drive, we don’t only get the opportunity to bring funds into the station, but we also get to spend this time highlighting the incredible programming and DJs we’ve had for over 25 years and counting.

There are two ways to donate: you can donate to receive gifts, or you can donate to receive a charitable gift certificate! Every $5.00 donated allots you 1 entry into our raffle!
